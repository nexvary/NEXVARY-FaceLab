# NEXVARY FaceLab — Stage 2500

Introduces Heritage & Landmarks Studio for Egypt, Türkiye and Algeria.

- 16 curated landmark presets based on official UNESCO-listed heritage locations and major heritage groupings.
- Dedicated Android Heritage screen with country/site selection.
- Portrait, Full Body, Travel and Cinematic composition modes.
- Day, Sunset and Night lighting modes.
- User-supplied/licensed landmark background image import.
- Self-hosted `/v1/heritage/catalog` and `/v1/heritage/composite` endpoints.
- Local rembg foreground extraction + compositing; no paid inference API.
- Deliberately does not bundle unlicensed Google/web photographs. Production photo packs must have documented redistribution rights.
